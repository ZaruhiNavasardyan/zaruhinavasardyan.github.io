Zaruhi Navasardyan

Researcher · Metric AI Lab · Yerevan

I am a researcher at Metric AI Lab in Yerevan, Armenia. The lab specialises in Physical AI, and my own work is mainly on robotic failure detection — revealing the earlier possible signs of failure, understand when and why the robots fail, develop systems to capture that.

My research also includes text embeddings and retrieval for low-resource languages, especially Armenian, where limited training data remains a central challenge.

  • Sep 2026 I will be at ECCV 2026 in Malmö, 8–12 September. Happy to talk to anyone working on runtime monitoring of robot policies, world models, or how embodied systems get evaluated.
  • Sep 2026 FailBench is on arXiv. It gathers 2,197 real and simulated manipulation attempts from 14 datasets, most of which were never built for failure detection, and asks how well vision-language models judge whether the robot succeeded. We tested thirteen detectors including general-purpose and specialist VLMs, top score is 0.77, measured in balanced accuracy, errors especially concentrated in the contact-rich, hard to see from visual input.
  • Aug 2026 Our work on physical AI benchmark audit and meta-analysis is on arXiv. From a 51-model by 12-benchmark score matrix, a greedy selection keeps four of the twelve and recovers 78.5% of the utility; dropping the redundant pairs reshuffles 22 of 51 models by three or more places.
  • Apr 2026 We put out ArmBench-LLM, a public leaderboard for how well frontier models — closed and open source — solve tasks in Armenian.
  • Mar 2026 I presented our Less is More paper on adapting text embeddings for low-resource languages at LoResLM, co-located with EACL 2026 in Rabat.
  • Mar 2026 We released ATE-2, our Armenian text-embedding models and the accompanying ArmBench-TextEmbed benchmark.
